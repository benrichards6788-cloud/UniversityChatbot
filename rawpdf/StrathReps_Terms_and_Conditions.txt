1 Introduction 
 
This document outlines the terms and conditions for Programme Representatives using the 
StrathReps system. 
2 Data Use 
 
As a student, your information will be used to show you the correct rep that represents you.  
 
All candidates who wish to nominate themselves should be aware of the following:  
• If you wish, your Pegasus picture will be displayed to students you would represent  and 
relevant department staff  
• Your supporting statement will be displayed to students who you would represent 
• Your Strathclyde email will be shared with Strath Union, to enable them to invite you to 
rep training 
• Your name will be displayed to students who you would represent 
• The students who you would represent, and other relevant reps will be able to access your 
Strathclyde email 
 
For more details about how the University uses your personal data please see the privacy notice 
for registered students. The University will process personal data in compliance with its Data 
Protection Policy.  More information on Data Protection can be  found on the University Data 
Protection  webpage. If you have any specific enquiries please contact the Information 
Governance Unit (IGU). 
3 Roles and Definitions  
 
▪ StrathReps refers to all student representatives at Strath Union 
 
▪ Student Academic Representatives refers to student reps who primarily advocate and 
support students on academic and related issues such as assessment and feedback, 
timetabling amongst others. Examples of academic reps include: Faculty, Departmental / 
School, Post -Graduate Research, Graduate Appren ticeship and Programme 
Representatives. 
 
▪ Programme Representatives represent all students in the year of their programme. They 
serve as the first point of call for students with issues within their programme. These 
include representatives for part -time, gr aduate apprentices, degree apprentices and 
distance learners. Programme representatives were previously referred to as class 
representatives, programme representatives therefore represent all modules they study.

Programme representatives will work closely with programme and module leaders to 
resolve issues and provide feedback for the modules within their programme 
 
4 Eligibility 
 
To be eligible to stand as a Programme Representative you must be a registered student of 
University of Strathclyde and be registered on the Programme you are standing for. 
5 Number of Programme Representatives 
 
Other than some exceptions, a maximum of 5 slots will be allocated to each year of a programme, 
based on the number of students: 
▪ 0-49 students: 2 Programme Representatives 
▪ 50-99 students: 3 Programme Representatives 
▪ 100-149 students: 4 Programme Representatives 
▪ 150+ students: 5 Programme Representatives 
6 Nomination 
 
Students will be able to nominate themselves on the StrathReps System by entering a supporting 
statement and accepting these terms and conditions. 
 
Supporting statements should outline why the candidate believes they would be a good rep.  
 
Supporting statements should not be written in an inappropriate or offensive, if so they would be 
in breach of the University Dignity and Respect Policy and will be dealt with accordingly.   
7 Automatic Appointment 
 
If there are the same number or less of candidates as positions available, the candidates will be 
appointed without an election.  
8 Election Method 
 
Elections will use the Single Transferable Vote (STV) electoral system. 
 
Students will be able to rank candidates in order of preference  and vote s will be distributed 
accordingly with the  STV methodology. They can rank as many or as few  candidates as they 
wish.

9 Timescales 
 
Timescales will be posted on the website 
 
Notifications will be sent to students at key points of the process. 
10 Withdrawing as a Rep 
 
Reps reserve the right to withdraw from their post for any reason  and can do this by emailing 
strathunion.rep@strath.ac.uk 
11 Vacant Positions 
 
A position can become vacant by a rep withdrawing in the system, or if a rep’s  registration 
changes to a status such as ‘withdrawn’ from studies.    
 
Strath Union Student Voice Coordinator (strathunion.rep@strath.ac.uk) and a nominated 
departmental contact will be contacted to agree whether to trigger another nominations and 
elections period.  
12 Tie Breaks 
 
In the unlikely event of a tie break, a winner will be randomly selected by the system  
13 Complaints about Election Process 
 
Students (including candidates or reps) may submit a complaint about the election process to 
strathunion.rep@strath.ac.uk with supporting evidence by completing the following form.  
  
14 Complaints about a Rep 
 
Students may also complain about their current rep by contacting the Strath Union Student Voice 
Coordinator (strathunion.rep@strath.ac.uk) 
 
15 Campaigning  
 
Candidates may choose to campaign but will not receive any reimbursement for campaigning 
material. When campaigning candidates should adhere to Strath Union’s election rules
