Procedure for returning marks and disseminating results  
Scope   
1. This document provides procedures for staff to follow in developing a consistent approach to the 
handling of marks for individual summative assessments and for total module marks. It outlines 
how marks should be recorded, released to students, prepared for, and ratified by Boards of 
Examiners prior to their approved module result being returned to Student Business for 
dissemination.  
2. These guidelines form part of the University Regulations. This document should be read in 
conjunction with other relevant documents. The University website contains policies and 
procedures on a range of assessment related matters, including: the Assessment and Feedback 
Policy.  
3. The Procedures noted here shall apply to all taught elements of undergraduate and postgraduate 
taught degrees from August 2024.   
Definitions   
4. For clarity, the following terminology is used throughout this procedure:   
a) Individual assessment mark: the mark achieved by a single piece of assessment as 
determined by the marker(s)   
b) Module mark: all individual assessment marks achieved by a single student in a single 
module, appropriately weighted and combined to produce a single overall integer mark and 
submitted to the Board of Examiners for consideration.   
c) Module result: the ratified mark for a module awarded to a student by the Board of Examiners. 
d) Mark Returner: the person with responsibility for collating and submitting the module marks 
and returning them to the student record for ratification by the Board of Examiners.  
 
Recording marks from summative assessments   
5. All individual assessment marks should be recorded within the relevant module pages in Myplace. 
This includes marks for assessments that are not practical to submit online to Myplace, such as 
marks for presentations, oral assessments, examinations, practical demonstrations, etc.

Releasing assessment marks to students   
6. All individual assessment marks shall be released to students via Myplace, in accordance with the 
Assessment and Feedback Policy. 
7. Where possible, marks on individual assessments should be returned to students online via 
Myplace, and normally within 15 days working days from submission deadline or extended 
deadline, with the exception of submissions which require double marking, such as dissertations or 
projects, as set out in the Assessment and Feedback Policy.  
   
Returning module marks for the preparation of marks schedules and Exam Board 
Schedules    
8. The Module Coordinator is responsible for: ensuring the calculated composite module mark that is 
obtained by each student for the module is correct prior to returning the module marks to the Marks 
Returner for transmission to the Student Record (PEGASUS) via Myplace. 
9. A deadline for the return of module marks is set for each formal assessment period and is 
published in the Calendar of Dates. 
10. Module marks will be returned to the Student Record by electronic transfer via Myplace and are 
undertaken by a designated staff member with a Mark Returner system role.     
 
Publication of module results   
 
Module Results: Marks ratified by Board of Examiners    
11. On receipt of finalised Post-Board Schedules, the module results are released by Student Business 
to students via PEGASUS and may be displayed on other mediums as appropriate (e.g. the 
University’s mobile app).     
Module Marks: Unapproved Marks   
12. Unapproved module marks (once returned to PEGASUS via Myplace) will be available on 
PEGASUS to students until Board of Examiners are due to meet. 
13. Students shall be advised that these module marks will not be approved until the appropriate Board 
of Examiners have met.   
   
Monitoring of results   
14. The Quality Assurance Committee scrutinises the pass rates for both postgraduate and 
undergraduate modules in all years except final honours years when it looks at the distribution of 
degree classifications.
